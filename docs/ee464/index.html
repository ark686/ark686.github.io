Click HERE for example video of TurtleBot4 navigating a maze in Gazebo:

  1. Overall space is the Hardy Lab space, (0, 0) in bottom right corner.
  2. Through trial-and-error, ended up deciding on a 6 x 9 "grid" (compromise between more maze paths and wide enough for easier navigation), but this is arbitrary.
  3. Navigation is a non-optimized right-wall follower in a ROS program. Note that wall followers are only guaranteed to work in "simply connected" mazes, where all walls are connected to the outer boundary. With a simply connected maze, a wall follower will transverse the entire maze (e.g., exhaustive search).
